05 / WHY DSV1

Built for both sides.

The product story is reduced to the characteristics that actually differentiate DSV1.

01

Dual-sided. Independently driven.

Each side runs on its own dedicated Android 11 SoC, allowing separate content playback without CMS splitting or an external player for each face.

02

Floor or ceiling.

A ceiling-mounted suspension frame is standard, while the rear chassis also supports floor-mounted and custom-built installations.

03

Brightness matched to the audience.

The outward-facing side is specified at 3,500 cd/m² and the inward-facing side at 700 cd/m², giving each environment its own brightness level.

Designed as one system, not two screens.

DSV1 combines dual display surfaces with independent Android systems, HDMI input, USB and RJ45 connectivity, 24/7 operation, and optional Windows-based configurations.
