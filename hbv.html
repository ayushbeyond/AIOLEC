06 / WHY HBV1

Built for demanding spaces.

A simple product story focused on visibility, installation flexibility and the platform underneath.

01

Visibility where it matters.

HBV1 is designed for environments where screens need to remain prominent and legible. The supplied hardware sheets specify 3,500 nits and support continuous unattended operation.

02

Install it your way.

Manual rotation supports 0°, 90°, 180° and 270°. The supplied drawings also show wall-mounted and suspended installation approaches across the range.

03

An open Android foundation.

Android 14 runs on RK3576 with 4GB + 32GB, Gigabit Ethernet, PoE, Wi-Fi 6 and Bluetooth 5.3, with remote monitoring, recovery and OTA or USB upgrades.

Designed to work with the systems around it.

HBV1 supports HDMI OUT, LVDS, V-by-One and MIPI-DSI display interfaces, standard audio I/O, USB expansion and optional multi-point touch technologies. The software stack supports common image and video formats and server or stand-alone operation.
